## What Chyweb sets up for your spa

- **A mobile booking website.** Guests can browse treatments, see prices and call reception. Tapping a treatment opens WhatsApp with its name already in the message.
- **Google Maps, local search and reviews.** We set up or update your Google Business Profile and optimise your treatment pages for searches in your area. Reception gets a review link to share after each visit.
- **Two promotional videos and a local ad campaign.** We turn photos or clips of your spa into two AI-assisted videos. A Facebook and Instagram campaign sends interested guests to your WhatsApp.
- **Ready-to-send WhatsApp replies.** We show reception how to use replies tailored to your spa to answer price questions, offer times, confirm appointments, send reminders and follow up.
- **A booking tracker.** Reception logs the treatment, the enquiry source when known, and whether the guest booked and attended. You can see which channels brought people through the door and who still needs a reply.
