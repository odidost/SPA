# Nera Spa

A fictional boutique day spa in Rayfield, Jos, Plateau State, Nigeria. The campaign offer is available year-round.

| Treatment | Duration | Guests | Total | What a guest can expect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Couples Massage | 60 minutes | Two | ₦60,000 | Two therapists and two beds in one private room. Each guest can choose their preferred pressure. |
| Signature Massage | 60 minutes | One | ₦32,000 | A full-body massage at the guest’s preferred pressure, with time to settle in and unwind. |
| Botanical Facial | 45 minutes | One | ₦24,000 | A gentle cleanse, a warm cotton compress and a moisturising finish. |

**Example address** · 6 Nera Courtyard, Rayfield, Jos, Plateau State.

**Hours in Jos** · Tuesday–Saturday, 10 am–7 pm. Sunday, noon–5 pm. Monday closed.

**Example guest message** · “Hi Nera, I’d like to book a Couples Massage for two. Which times are available?”

**Example reception reply** · “It’s ₦60,000 for both of you. You’ll each have a therapist in the same private room for 60 minutes. Which day suits you?”
